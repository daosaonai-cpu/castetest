Cybersecurity Awareness

A Guide for Students: Staying Safe in the Digital World

What is Cybersecurity?

  • Digital Armor: It's the practice of protecting your devices, personal information, and online identity.
  • Why it matters: Cybercriminals actively look for stolen info to use for scams, bullying, and theft.
  • Your role: You are the very first line of defense against online threats!

Password Power

  • Make it long: Use at least 12 characters. Nonsense phrases work best (e.g., Blue$Sky*Pizza99).
  • Keep it secret: Never share your passwords with friends—only with a parent or guardian.
  • Mix it up: Never reuse the same password for your school account, email, and games.

Spotting Phishing & Scams

  • What is it? Fake emails, texts, or messages pretending to be a trusted company or friend.
  • Red Flags: Urgent warnings, bad spelling, asking for passwords, or "You won a free phone!"
  • Action Plan: Think before you click! If it looks suspicious, delete it or ask a teacher.

Safe Social Media Habits

  • Privacy Check: Set profiles to private. Only accept requests from people you know in real life.
  • The Internet is Forever: Think carefully before posting photos or comments.
  • Location tagging: Turn off location sharing so strangers don't know exactly where you are.

Device Defense

  • Lock it down: Always lock your phone or laptop screen with a PIN or password when you step away.
  • Update regularly: Keep your apps and operating system up to date. Those updates fix hidden security holes!
  • Download safely: Only download apps and games from official stores (like the App Store or Google Play).

Public Wi-Fi Perils

  • It's not private: Free Wi-Fi at cafes, airports, or malls can easily be snooped on by hackers.
  • Avoid sensitive sites: Never log into your bank, school portal, or social media on unsecured public networks.
  • Use a VPN: If you must use public Wi-Fi, a Virtual Private Network scrambles your data to keep it safe.

Digital Citizenship

  • Be kind online: Treat others with the same respect you would in person. Behind every screen is a real person.
  • Don't engage: If someone is cyberbullying you, don't argue back. Block them and take screenshots for proof.
  • Speak up: Always tell a trusted adult or teacher if you or a friend feels unsafe online.

Stay Safe Online!

  • Remember to stop, think, and connect carefully.
